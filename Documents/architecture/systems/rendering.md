# Rendering

## Overview

URP rendering extensions. The selection pick pass ([selection](selection.md)) is gameplay-critical for interaction targeting. The atmospherics pass ([atmospherics](atmospherics.md)) composites gas scatter, plasma glow, and heat distortion from sim GPU textures via `AtmosRenderContext`. **Decal Renderer** is enabled on the forward renderer (Use Rendering Layers on) for health blood decals and future surface marks — see `DecalRenderingLayers`. **Atmos snapshots are server-built** — hosts render directly; pure clients consume Phase 1 dirty-chunk patches through `AtmosClientVisualizationBridge`. Late-join bootstrap / AOI remain Phase 2; see [atmospherics](atmospherics.md).

Station materials use `STDefault` (shader `SS3D/Station`; "Simple Toon/SToon Default" until 2026-11-18 — materials bind it by GUID and no code finds it by name), rewritten 2026-11-17 as a **flat-with-lighting** shader: `albedo × (ambient × AO + lights) + emission`, lighting in `StationFlatLighting.hlsl` (wrapped diffuse from all URP lights with their shadows and an optional range-relative falloff, rolled off so pools don't clip). No toon bands, ramps or per-material lighting knobs; the look is tuned globally by the `StationShading` volume override. `STTransparent` (`SS3D/Station Transparent`, glass) uses the same lighting in ForwardLit; the half-toon includes and `STOutline` were deleted 2026-11-18. `STDefault`'s **Alpha Clip** toggle (`_ALPHATEST_ON`, `_Cutoff` against albedo alpha × colour alpha) clips every pass, shadows included. Steel floor tiles (`TileGrey`, `TileWhite`, `TileGreyDark`, `TileKitchen`, `TileBar`) use `StationFloorTile` (`SS3D/Station Floor Tile`, 2026-11-18): tiles, thin grout lines and a grout diamond on each cell centre are drawn procedurally in mesh UV space (UV 0..1 = one cell = 2 × 2 tiles, as on `FloorTile.fbx`, the airlock half tiles and the floor tile item), optionally checkered, and lit by `StationFlat.hlsl`'s own passes. The old textures drew grout about three times as thick. Against the textured `STDefault` floor it measured +0.069 ms (±0.005) in the opaque pass at 3840×2160, with floors covering 20 % of the frame (about 0.04 ms per million floor pixels), plus about 0.04 ms in the fixture shadow pass, probably because floors no longer share `STDefault`'s SRP batches (RTX 3070, High, four A/B pairs, 2026-11-18). No station `.mat` is on URP Lit: the eye, slot machine, blood trail, tile overlays and M4 moved over 2026-11-18, and floor stripes/decals (`FloorVisualMesh`) clone `Resources/FloorVisuals/FloorVisualCutout.mat`. The shaders have `UnityPerMaterial` + `#pragma multi_compile_instancing` — Metastation wins come from enabling material instancing and keeping MeshRenderers free of permanent MaterialPropertyBlocks (see [srp-batcher-gpu-instancing](../2026-07_srp-batcher-gpu-instancing.md)). Palette emission must sample `_EmissionMap` (same UV swatch pattern as albedo) — a flat `_EmissionColor` alone washes shared `PaletteEmission` materials white. `STDefault` includes DepthOnly + DepthNormals (with `_WRITE_RENDERING_LAYERS`) so URP Decal Layers can distinguish characters from tiles, and ForwardLit samples DBuffer (`ApplyDecalToBaseColor`) so health blood / surface `DecalProjector`s tint skin and worn clothing. **Ambient occlusion is CORTAO**, not URP SSAO: `CortaoAmbientOcclusionFeature` runs CORTAO's compute passes (prepass, main, filter) after the DepthNormals prepass, then a separable bilateral blur, and publishes `_SS3DOcclusionTexture`. The `StationShading` volume override (Ambient, AmbientOcclusion, LightStrength, LightWrap, LightRolloff, LightOcclusion, LightContrast, LightFalloff) is published as `_SS3DStationAmbient` / `_SS3DStationLights` / `_SS3DStationFalloff` by `StationShadingFeature`, a pass-less renderer feature kept apart from AO so turning CORTAO off doesn't change the look; the defaults (`DefaultVolumeProfile` and the C# constants, used by the lobby profile, Scene view and previews) are ambient 0, light strength 4, wrap 1, rolloff 1, light occlusion 0.4, light contrast 2.5, light falloff 1. `SS3D_GameplayVolumeProfile` keeps ambient 0, wrap 1 and falloff 1 but runs light strength 3.92, rolloff 0.83, light occlusion 0.283 and light contrast 1. Fixtures alone light the station like old SS3D and the gaps between pools go dark. At rolloff 1 a fully lit surface sits flat at its albedo ("unlit + lighting"); the gameplay rolloff of 0.83 levels off at about 1.2× albedo instead. There is no tonemapping. The gameplay grade is post-exposure 0.93, contrast −16, a faint warm colour filter, bloom threshold 0.4 / intensity 0.5 and vignette 0.166. This diverges from [design §1/§8](../../design/rendering-lighting.md#8-post-processing-stack) (fixture-driven light, AO deprioritized) at the owner's request, 2026-11-17. With Ambient at 0 (2026-11-18) the station is fixture-lit only, so rooms still go dark when power fails. Emission stays unaffected. DepthNormals also serves DBuffer + Decal Layers.

CORTAO's cost is set per quality tier (off on Low, half resolution with a bilateral upsample on Medium, full resolution on High/Ultra), and so are the fixture spot shadows (atlas, per-light resolution, soft filter). The renderer is Forward+, so on every tier each pixel is lit by every fixture in range. Settings and measured GPU costs: [rendering-quality-tiers](rendering-quality-tiers.md).

Client FOV / fog of war (`VisionRendererFeature` and its mask and composite passes) belongs to the [fov](fov.md) map: walls drawn whole from a raycast map, an inward-only soft edge, and people shown whole or not at all.

## Extension points

- New render features: add URP `ScriptableRendererFeature` under `Rendering/URP/`.
- Station shader with its own albedo: `#define STATION_CUSTOM_INPUT`, declare the inputs `StationFlat.hlsl` lists (textures, `UnityPerMaterial`) plus a `Station_Albedo(meshUV)`, then include `StationFlat.hlsl` in every pass. Albedo that depends on world position also defines `STATION_ALBEDO_WS` and a `Station_AlbedoWS(meshUV, positionWS)`, which the forward pass calls instead; keep per-map data in globals, not `UnityPerMaterial`, so the SRP Batcher keeps batching. Lighting, shadows, CORTAO, DBuffer decals and Decal Layers come with it; `StationFloorTile.hlsl` is the example.
- Outline / auxiliary meshes that must not participate in pick: set rendering layer `SelectionRenderingLayers.ExcludeFromSelectionPick`.
- World surface marks: stamp `DecalRenderingLayers.ReceiveWorldDecals` on receiver renderers; point floor `DecalProjector`s at `WorldFloorProjectorMask`. Custom opaque shaders must implement DepthNormals with `_WRITE_RENDERING_LAYERS` or Decal Layers will not exclude them, and must sample DBuffer (`ApplyDecalToBaseColor` / `_DBUFFER_MRT*`) or DBuffer projectors never tint albedo (worn jumpsuit / ST floors).

## Pitfalls

- **`UNITY_MATRIX_I_VP` already holds the render-target flip:** only the camera's own matrix needs clip Y flipped by hand. Flipping both mirrored every atmos ray across the screen's horizontal centre line, so gas showed as clouds in space and held upside-down silhouettes, while the debug views stayed correct because they use the camera matrix. `AtmosScatterPlacementTests` renders through the real feature. It needs a graphics device, so it skips in headless CI. Hit 2026-11-06.
- **AO noise boils without TAA:** CORTAO expects a new seed every frame and TAA to resolve it. `CortaoAmbientOcclusionFeature` reseeds only when the camera's AA is TAA and holds a fixed seed otherwise (SMAA/FXAA/off, Scene view), so the noise stays still instead of shimmering. The player's AA setting overrides the prefab's TAA at runtime (below). CORTAO's own filter still left visible grain (owner, 2026-11-17), so a bilateral blur (per-tier `BlurRadius`, 6 screen px) follows it; it rejects samples off the centre's surface plane rather than by raw depth, or grazing floors would stop blurring. URP SSAO's blue noise had the same problem under SMAA (2026-11-05).
- **Light leaks through walls if made omnidirectional:** an unshadowed light (the fixtures' PointFills, until 2026-11-18) is not stopped by walls. Station lighting is wrapped diffuse that stays zero on back faces; keep `LightWrap` below 1 rather than adding an omni term.
- **Do not move station AO back to `_SCREEN_SPACE_OCCLUSION`:** URP strips that keyword's variants from player builds unless its own SSAO feature is active (`ShaderBuildPreprocessor` skips inactive features). The station shaders sample `_SS3DOcclusionTexture` unconditionally; the feature binds white when AO is off, so an unset global never reads as grey. `STTransparent` sets `receiveOcclusion = false`: it is not in the depth-normals prepass, so the texture under it belongs to what is behind.
- **CORTAO on DX11/SM5:** the reference wrappers need SM 6.7 integer sampling. The packed buffer is R32_SFloat holding uint bits, the occluder table is a `StructuredBuffer` indexed by hand, and the off-screen border is tested in code. `flipY` stays false because URP renders depth/normals flipped on UV-starts-at-top APIs. OpenGL and Vulkan compile `RWTexture2D<float>` to an `r32f` image that must match the bound texture, so the occlusion textures are R32 there and R8 only on D3D/Metal (`GetOcclusionFormat`).
- **Changing `StationShading`'s C# defaults changes nothing in the Editor:** URP's `Assets/DefaultVolumeProfile.asset` stores a copy of every volume component, written with the defaults the type had when it first appeared, and that copy is the baseline under every profile (the lobby profile has no Station Shading override). Edit the default profile, the gameplay profile, the C# constants and the shader fallback together. Hit 2026-11-17: two brightness passes showed "almost no change" because the stored 0.85 / 1.5 never moved.
- **Lit areas keep grading instead of reading flat:** rolloff turns light into light / (1 + k·light), which levels off at 1/k. Below k = 1 the ceiling sits above the albedo and pools grade toward hot spots; at k = 1 a fully lit surface flattens to its albedo (the unlit look), and Light Strength then grows pools rather than brightening them. `LightStrength` is clamped to its slider range, so a value past the max silently caps. Hit 2026-11-18.
- **URP light falloff cannot reproduce the old SS3D look:** old SS3D (Built-in pipeline) attenuated lights relative to their range, about 1 / (1 + 25 (d/range)²), so fixtures filled rooms with broad pools and the gaps between them read as dark patches with no ambient at all. URP is physical 1/d²: a hot spot at the fixture, gone within a few metres. `StationShading.LightFalloff` (0 = URP, 1 = range-relative) rebuilds the Built-in falloff per pixel in `Station_AdditionalLightAttenuation` from URP's per-light data (`attenuation.x` = 1/range²), keeping URP's spot cone and shadows. Range-relative light is far stronger at distance, so retune LightStrength when switching. Hit 2026-11-18.
- **Fixture light reads as a flat wash, not pools:** wide, long-range wall spots overlap and even out, and unshadowed fill lights flatten it further. Pools only show when ambient is low relative to light strength and wrap/rolloff stay small (ambient 0.34 + light 0.64 showed no pools; 0.2 + 2.2 with wrap/rolloff 0.1 projects them). For darker gaps, keep spot shadow strength 1 (0.85 leaked 15% of the light into shadows), keep spot range short and add no fills: since 2026-11-18 each fixture is one 6.12 m spot (was 10–12 m plus a PointFill). `LightContrast` above 1 (light^γ) sinks remaining spill. Negative grading contrast lifts blacks, the gaps included; the gameplay profile runs −16. Hit 2026-11-17.
- **Station shaders render black without published globals:** the look has no fixture-only fallback, so `_SS3DStationAmbient.w` flags that the feature published values; when it is 0 (a renderer without `StationShadingFeature`, material previews) `StationFlatLighting.hlsl` uses `StationShading`'s defaults. Keep the two default sets in sync. Before 2026-11-18 the AO feature published these too, so unticking it left the look on whatever was published last; `StationShadingFeatureTests` checks the renderer keeps the feature active.
- **A renderer rebuild kills feature materials that `Dispose` doesn't null:** symptom: after a quality change in Play, or toggling or editing any renderer feature, hover/examine stop working (the pick target keeps old contents, so a stray wall examines now and then) and gas visuals vanish, with no error. Cause: all four URP assets share the renderer's feature objects; a rebuild disposes the old renderer's features and then calls `Create()` in the same frame, and in Play `CoreUtils.Destroy` is a deferred `Object.Destroy`, so `Create()` saw the doomed material as alive and kept it; at end of frame it died and `AddRenderPasses` skipped the pass from then on. Fix: null every material field after destroying it in `Dispose` (all features do now). `PlayerDisplayApplier` switches quality at Play start when the player's saved Quality differs from the Editor's, so an ordinary Host session can start broken. `RendererFeatureRebuildTests` and `SelectionHoverScenarios` guard it. Hit 2026-11-18.
- **Carpet/vendor surfaces break into streaks:** constant palette-swatch UVs produce zero derivatives in the derivative tangent frame (`Station_NormalWS`); unguarded `rsqrt(0)` creates NaN normals. Clamp the squared tangent length before reciprocal square root so flat normal maps retain geometric normals.
- **Rolloff softens shadows under bright fixtures:** `LightRolloff` compresses the summed light after shadows, so a partial shadow next to a hotspot loses contrast. Lower it (or `LightStrength`) if fixture shadows look washed out. Fixture spots cast soft shadows at the Medium resolution tier with the pipeline's soft-shadow quality, both set per URP asset ([rendering-quality-tiers](rendering-quality-tiers.md)). Low quality disables additional-light shadows at the pipeline level.
- **Atmos visuals outlive a paused sim:** `AtmosSubSystem.SimulationPaused` stops the tick, not the render path — the retained GPU snapshot keeps scatter/glow/distortion and `AtmosWindView` animating (and a remote client's map editor cannot pause the server). `AtmosRenderContext.SetRenderingSuppressed` is checked by both `AtmosRendererFeature.AddRenderPasses` and `AtmosWindView.Update`; `MapEditorSubSystem` owns the toggle and must clear the statically-held flag on destroy too. Hit 2026-11-04 (henkhooft/SS3D#147).
- **Decal technique is pinned to DBuffer, not Automatic:** `SS3D_ForwardPlusRenderer` Decal feature `technique: 1`. Automatic resolves through URP's cached `SHADER_API_MOBILE` check, which differs between the headless CI editor and a desktop player: v0.3.3's CI build kept only `DecalScreenSpaceProjector`, the desktop player chose DBuffer, found no `DBufferProjector` pass, and URP's `DecalDrawErrorSystem` drew every bullet hole and blood decal with the magenta error material (henkhooft/SS3D#111). Local builds kept DBuffer, so they never showed it. `Shader Graphs/Decal` and `Hidden/SS3D/MultiplyTint` also stay in Always Included Shaders. To check a player build, run the `assert_bullet_decal` automation opcode (fails if the DBuffer pass is missing) or `stamp_bullet_holes` + `screenshot`. Hit 2026-11-02.
- **Borderless below native resolution offsets the mouse:** `Screen.SetResolution` in `FullScreenWindow` at a smaller size stretches the swapchain to the desktop, but Linux/X11 still reports pointer coordinates in desktop pixels, so map editor and diegetic UI clicks land off-target. `PlayerDisplayApplier.ApplyWindow` always uses native size in Borderless; the saved resolution only applies to Fullscreen/Windowed. Hit 2026-10-31.
- **One URP asset per quality tier.** `QualitySettings` has four levels (Low/Medium/High/Ultra, default High), each pointing at its own `Assets/Settings/URP/SS3D_URPAsset_<Tier>.asset`; `GraphicsSettings` keeps Ultra as the fallback. A pipeline-wide change (renderer features, GPU Resident Drawer, HDR) must go into all four. Render scale and upscaler are written at runtime by `PlayerSettingsSubSystem` onto whichever asset is active ([2026-10_player-settings](../2026-10_player-settings.md)); in the editor it restores them when play stops, so don't treat play-mode values in the inspector as saved. All tiers share `SS3D_ForwardPlusRenderer`, so per-tier AO lives in `CortaoAmbientOcclusionFeature.QualityLevels`, matched by `QualitySettings` index ([rendering-quality-tiers](rendering-quality-tiers.md)).
- **Keep the renderer on Forward+, or lighting breaks at wall-mesh edges:** on Forward (`SS3D_ForwardPlusRenderer` `m_RenderingMode: 0`), URP shades each mesh with only its N most important lights. It ranks them by intensity and distance, not by which side of the mesh they light. When this was hit, each fixture was two lights with 10–12 m range, so 13–27 lights reached every tile (one 6.12 m spot per fixture since the 2026-11-18 retune). On Low (limit 2) and Medium (4), walls that kept only the fixture behind them rendered solid black, because ambient is 0. On every tier, neighbouring wall meshes kept different lights, so a character's shadow was cut off at a wall seam and panels showed brightness steps. The flat-lighting rewrite switched the renderer to Forward by accident (2026-11-18). It is Forward+ (`2`) again, as `URPFoundationSetup` writes it. `ForwardPlusLightingTests` guards the mode, and checks that custom lit shaders compile `_CLUSTER_LIGHT_LOOP`; without it, a shader gets no additional lights on Forward+ (`SteelWreckage.shader` lacked it). Cost and look: [rendering-quality-tiers](rendering-quality-tiers.md#lighting-path-forward). Hit 2026-11-18.
- **Player camera AA is overridden at runtime.** `PlayerCamera.prefab` serializes TAA, but the player's Anti-Aliasing setting is applied to every game camera without a target texture on `beginCameraRendering`. Render-texture cameras (preview booth, icon generator) keep their own AA.

- **Selection feature changed IDs on every script reload:** `SelectionPickRendererFeatureSetup.RemoveBrokenFeatures` destroyed healthy selection features as well as broken stubs. Keep valid instances, synchronize `m_RendererFeatureMap` to the actual sub-asset IDs and save only when wiring changes. `SelectionRendererSetupTests` asserts repeated setup preserves object identity and the recovery map.

- **Permanent MaterialPropertyBlocks break SRP Batcher:** any MPB on a MeshRenderer (e.g. old Selectable `_SelectionColor`, Intact white integrity tint) drops that draw out of the SRP Batcher path. Selection pick uses transient MPB on `DrawMesh` via `SelectionPickContext`; intact integrity clears MPBs. Hit 2026-07-28 (Metastation).
- **Light/reflection probes block GPU instancing:** BlendProbes (and reflection probes) inject per-renderer non-instanced data — Frame Debugger shows "Non-instanced properties set for instanced shader." SS3D does not use probe lighting; keep `LightProbeUsage` / `ReflectionProbeUsage` **Off** on Content renderers via **SS3D/Rendering/Run Content Prefab Recipes** (`RendererProbeUsageSetup`). Hit 2026-07-29.
- **Selection `DrawMesh` needs explicit frustum cull:** HashGrid AOI ≠ camera frustum. Pick collect must `TestPlanesAABB` against the request camera (`IsInPickFrustum`) or Metastation pays full-AOI pick draws. See [selection](selection.md). Hit 2026-07-28.
- **GPU Instancing needs the material flag:** ST shaders compile instancing variants, but assets need `enableInstancing` / `m_EnableInstancingVariants: 1`. Floor ST mats were flipped in [srp-batcher-gpu-instancing](../2026-07_srp-batcher-gpu-instancing.md).
- **Linux player: Vulkan, Graphics Jobs off, OpenGL opt-in:** Linux keeps Vulkan first so DBuffer decals work; URP's `DecalRendererFeature` rejects DBuffer on OpenGL ("Decal DBuffer technique is not supported with OpenGL"), so `-force-glcore` (the Linux zip's `play_ss3d_opengl.sh`) loses every bullet hole and blood decal. `m_GraphicsJobs` stays `0` for `LinuxStandaloneSupport`: v0.14.0 on Vulkan with native Graphics Jobs ran ~25 fps at 1440p on an RX 6950 XT, ~90 fps without them; OpenGL ran ~200 fps uncapped. The leftover gap is a Vulkan present stall under XWayland. `-force-wayland` is no fix: wrong monitor, tiny resolution, broken pointer coordinates. OpenGL with decals would need a ScreenSpace-technique renderer for GL devices and the station shader writing rendering layers in ForwardLit. Hit 2026-11-13.
- **GPU Resident Drawer on Linux/OpenGL:** `m_GPUResidentDrawerMode` must stay **Disabled** (`0`) on every quality-tier asset (`SS3D_URPAsset_Low/Medium/High/Ultra`). Instanced Drawing requires `BatchBufferTarget.RawBuffer`; unsupported APIs spam the warning every rebuild. Do not re-enable in `URPFoundationSetup` without checking the active graphics API. Do not confuse with classic GPU Instancing / SRP Batcher (still on).
- **ST meshes ignore blood/floor DecalProjectors:** the Decal technique is DBuffer. `STDefault` ForwardLit must keep `#pragma multi_compile_fragment _ _DBUFFER_MRT1 _DBUFFER_MRT2 _DBUFFER_MRT3` and `ApplyDecalToBaseColor` under `#ifdef _DBUFFER` — calling it without the keyword samples an unbound buffer (weight 0 → black mesh). DepthNormals alone only fixes Decal Layers filtering, not albedo tint.
- **Decal Layers must stay enabled:** `SS3D_ForwardPlusRenderer` Decal feature `decalLayers: 1`. Floor/bullet projectors target `ReceiveWorldDecals` only; characters stay Default. Turning layers off makes Lit tiles look fine while masking breaks for the intended filter path — do not disable during lighting retunes.
- **Do not drop DepthNormals when disabling AO:** DBuffer decals + Decal Layers require the DepthNormals prepass (`_WRITE_RENDERING_LAYERS`). CORTAO is another consumer; even with it off the ~opaque-sized DepthNormals pass remains. Removing it re-breaks floor blood painting through the player ([health](health.md)). Hit 2026-07-29 (Frame Debugger).
- **AO missing on a shader:** only shaders that sample `_SS3DOcclusionTexture` get CORTAO occlusion; URP Lit, Simple Lit and other custom shaders got none once URP SSAO was removed (2026-11-17). Every station `.mat` is on the station shaders since 2026-11-18, but an FBX's embedded import material is URP Lit and is easy to miss: `M4.prefab` rendered with the M4 FBX's `Palette05` until it was pointed at `Palette.mat`. Still outside: `SubstanceMaterial.mat` (URP Simple Lit; water cooler, cryotube, mug) and `SteelWreckage.shader` (no DepthNormals). A new opaque shader also needs a DepthNormals pass, or it is invisible to CORTAO as an occluder.
- **Alpha Clip enabled from code is stripped from builds:** `_ALPHATEST_ON` is a `shader_feature_local`, so a material that only calls `EnableKeyword` at runtime gets the unclipped variant in a player (solid quads). Clone a material asset that ships with the keyword on: `FloorVisualMesh.CreateCutoutMaterial` clones `Resources/FloorVisuals/FloorVisualCutout.mat`. It used to build URP Lit through `Shader.Find` + `EnableKeyword`.
- **URP's `ShadowCasterPass.hlsl` reads Lit's inputs:** with `_ALPHATEST_ON` it samples `_BaseMap` / `_BaseColor`, which the station shaders don't have. `STDefault` uses its own `StationShadowVertex` / `StationShadowFragment` in `StationFlat.hlsl`; keep its shadow bias and clamping in step with URP's pass on URP upgrades.
- **GPU instancing still limited after probes Off:** Frame Debugger on Metastation — after Content probe clear + GenericShadeless instancing, remaining breaks are mostly different adjacency/door meshes and intentional emissive MPBs; do not expect floor-like instance counts on airlocks. Hit 2026-07-29.
- **Floor tile icons still show the old texture:** `IconPreviewGenerator` renders every material as `_MainTex` × `_Color` through `ObjectIcon`, never through the floor shader. `StationFloorTile` doesn't draw `_MainTex`, so the steel floor materials keep their old tile texture there for icons only (old thick grout in the map editor palette and floor-tile item icons). If you recolour a floor material, change that texture too or the icon drifts.
- **Item/tile icons depend on scene lighting:** do not render icons with live station materials or bare scene lights. Use `IconPreviewGenerator` (`Unlit/ObjectIcon` overrides). `RuntimePreviewGenerator` still spawns temporary preview lights as a fallback path — do not rely on scene lighting. Keep `ObjectIcon` in Always Included Shaders (same pattern as InteractionOutline).
- **Shiny player head under close point lights:** close URP point lights create a bright N·L hotspot on bald/curved meshes (bloom amplifies it). The station shader has no specular, and `LightRolloff` flattens hotspots; move or dim the light rather than copying Built-in intensities.
