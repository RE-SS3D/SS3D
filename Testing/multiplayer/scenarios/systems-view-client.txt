# Client side of mppm:systems-view: joins the running round, opens the map editor, turns the Atmos view on and
# receives its structure, traces the valve the host shut and primed (both sides, labelled), then sees the two traced
# pressures close at least half their gap after the valve opens, in the live numbers and in the trace panel. It is not
# an admin, so it has no Pause and the one it asks for anyway is refused; then it watches the host admin's pause: Paused
# in the live numbers and the panel, the gap holding while nothing steps and moving with the host's steps, and Paused
# gone once the host's editor closes.
# No screenshot: the MPPM clone's game view is a strip too small to show the panel; the visual pilot covers the look.
wait_timeout 60
wait_connected
wait_round Ongoing
wait_seconds 1
ready
embark
assert_embarked
open_map_editor
systems_view atmos
assert_pause_refused
trace_valve
assert_trace_pressure_change
assert_trace_paused_step
close_map_editor
disconnect
