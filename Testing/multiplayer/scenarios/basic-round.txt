# Dedicated-server side of the basic-round scenario. A real UNITY_SERVER build has no
# ClientManager connection of its own, so this side only observes: wait for the transport to
# come up, watch the round reach Ongoing once the client starts it, then stay up long enough
# for Ongoing to replicate and for the client to embark/console/disconnect before we tear down.
# Disconnecting immediately after Ongoing races the SyncVar and leaves the client stuck on
# wait_round (see networking-session.md Pitfalls).
wait_connected
wait_round Ongoing
wait_seconds 30
disconnect
